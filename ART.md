# Battle Drones — Art Guide

Everything the game draws lives under `public/assets/sprites/` (plus PWA
icons in `public/icons/`). The current PNGs are **programmer placeholders**
generated by `npm run sprites` (`tools/gen-sprites.mjs`). To ship real art,
**replace the PNG files in place, keeping the exact dimensions and frame
layout** described below — no code changes needed.

## Global conventions

- Art is authored at **64 px per world tile** ("HD"); the engine renders
  tiles at 64 px and defaults the camera to 0.5× zoom, so full detail
  shows when zoomed in. Pixel-art, transparent backgrounds.
- The game renders with `pixelArt: true` (nearest-neighbour scaling) — no
  anti-aliased edges needed; crisp pixels look best.
- Frame grids are strict: frame *n* sits at
  `x = (n % cols) * frameW, y = floor(n / cols) * frameH`.
- **Direction convention:** sheets with 8 rotation frames use
  frame `i` = facing `i × 45°` **clockwise, starting at North (up)**:

  | frame | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | faces | N | NE | E | SE | S | SW | W | NW |

- Two factions with identical layouts: `blue/` (player) and `red/` (enemy).
  Silhouettes should match between factions; only accent colors change.
- Damage/rubble states: `idle` is full health, `damaged` shows at ≤ 50% HP,
  `rubble` is the corpse left after destruction.

## File inventory

### Units — `assets/sprites/units/{blue,red}/`

| File | Sheet size | Frame | Layout |
|---|---|---|---|
| `harvester.png` | 512×128 | 64×64 | 8 dirs per row; **row 0 = empty, row 1 = loaded with scrap** |
| `scout.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs |
| `stinger.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs |
| `flak-crawler.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs |
| `wasp.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs |
| `raptor.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs |

`units/shadow.png` (64×64, single frame, shared): soft dark ellipse drawn
under air units at ground level so altitude reads. Keep it a translucent
blob — it's tinted/faded in engine.

Unit personality cheat-sheet (see DESIGN.md for stats):

- **Harvester** — chunky industrial hauler, visible cargo bay (the loaded
  row should clearly show scrap in the bay).
- **Scout** — tiny fragile quadcopter, big sensor eye.
- **Stinger** — cheap tracked gun drone, single forward barrel.
- **Flak Crawler** — squat tracked platform, twin AA barrels + radar dish.
- **Wasp** — aggressive dart-shaped strike drone, forward sting.
- **Raptor** — sleek air-superiority interceptor, swept wings.

### Buildings — `assets/sprites/buildings/{blue,red}/`

| File | Sheet size | Frame | Frames (cols) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hq.png` | 576×192 | 192×192 | idle, damaged, rubble |
| `fab-plant.png` | 384×128 | 128×128 | idle, damaged, rubble |
| `airpad.png` | 384×128 | 128×128 | idle, damaged, rubble |
| `turret-base.png` | 64×64 | 64×64 | single static frame |
| `turret-head.png` | 512×64 | 64×64 | 8 dirs (rotates in engine on the base) |

Footprints: HQ 3×3 tiles, Fab Plant / Airpad 2×2, Turret 1×1. Buildings
should visually fill their frame minus a ~6 px margin.

### Terrain — `assets/sprites/terrain/tiles.png`

Single row of 8 tiles, 64×64 each, sheet 512×64:

| index | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tile | ground A | ground B | ground C | rock A | rock B | scrap full | scrap half | scrap empty |

Ground variants are interchangeable filler; rocks must read as impassable
to ground vehicles; scrap tiles deplete full → half → empty during play.
Tiles butt against each other — keep edges tileable (no baked borders).

### FX — `assets/sprites/fx/`

| File | Sheet size | Frame | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `muzzle.png` | 64×64 | 64×64 | single flash frame, shown ~1 tick |
| `bolt.png` | 64×64 | 64×64 | projectile/beam drawn **pointing North**; rotated in engine |
| `explosion.png` | 384×64 | 64×64 | 6 frames: fireball (0–3) → smoke (4–5) |

Selection rings, health bars, fog of war, and rally markers are drawn
programmatically in engine — no art needed.

### PWA icons — `public/icons/`

`icon-192.png` (192×192) and `icon-512.png` (512×512). Full-bleed square,
dark background (used as maskable icon too, so keep the subject inside the
center ~80%).

## Regenerating placeholders

`npm run sprites` rewrites the placeholder files deterministically. Files
listed in `REAL_ART` in `tools/gen-sprites.mjs` are skipped — **add every
real sprite to that list when you drop it in** so the generator can never
clobber finished art.

Real art so far (from the imagegen experiments; red faction variants are
generated from the blue originals with `node tools/recolor-red.mjs`): all
units, all buildings, terrain tiles, all fx, and the PWA icons.

Still placeholder: `units/shadow.png` only.
